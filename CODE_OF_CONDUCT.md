# Code of Conduct

Babel Fish should be a useful and respectful open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while still addressing problems clearly.
- Respect different backgrounds, languages, time zones, and experience levels.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask you to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block users when behavior harms the project community.

To report a concern, open a private security advisory if sensitive information is involved, or contact the project maintainer through GitHub.
